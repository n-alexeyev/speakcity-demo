Speak City Phrase Cards

One line per card. The Spanish word being spoken lights up yellow in the video export.

Format: Spanish (English) /pro-NUN-see-AY-shun/. Keep one pronunciation chunk per Spanish word, separated by spaces, so the yellow dot lands under the right word.

PNG and video are 1080×1920 with a transparent background (the preview background is not exported unless you pick green screen). The video records in real time, so keep this tab open while it saves. Chrome and Firefox save WebM with transparency (CapCut and DaVinci read it); Safari saves MP4 without transparency, so pick green screen there.
